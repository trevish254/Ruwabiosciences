Ingredients

Every component works in harmony to nourish, protect, and restore your skin’s natural balance. Discover the key ingredients that make our products truly transformative.

Azelaic Acid

We use azelaic acid for its ability to brighten skin, unclog pores, and reduce redness. Naturally derived from grains, it helps refine texture and combat blemishes, making it ideal for sensitive and acne-prone skin.

Peptides

Peptides boost collagen production, improving skin firmness and reducing fine lines. By supporting hydration and elasticity, they help keep skin plump, resilient, and youthful.

Niacinamide

A skincare powerhouse, niacinamide strengthens the skin barrier, balances oil production, and minimizes pores. It helps calm redness, improve clarity, and protect against environmental stressors.

Ceramides

Essential for a strong skin barrier, ceramides help lock in moisture and protect against environmental stressors. We use them to support hydration, prevent dryness, and keep skin soft and resilient.

Aloe Vera

Aloe vera is a natural hydrator known for its soothing and healing properties. We use it to calm irritation, reduce redness, and deliver lightweight moisture for a fresh, healthy complexion.

Green Tea Extract

Packed with antioxidants, green tea extract soothes inflammation, fights free radical damage, and helps reduce redness. It’s a gentle yet effective ingredient for calm, refreshed, and protected skin.

Squalane

A lightweight yet deeply nourishing oil, squalane mimics the skin’s natural lipids to lock in moisture and strengthen the barrier. It’s fast-absorbing, non-greasy, and perfect for restoring softness and balance.

Hyaluronic Acid

We use hyaluronic acid for its exceptional ability to hydrate and plump the skin. This moisture-binding ingredient helps maintain elasticity, smooth fine lines, and leave skin looking dewy and refreshed.

Vitamin C

Vitamin C is a powerful antioxidant that brightens the complexion and supports collagen production. We include it to help protect against free radicals and promote a more even glow.
